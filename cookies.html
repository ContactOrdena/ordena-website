What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. They help websites remember your preferences and understand how you use the site.

How Ordena uses cookies

ordena.co.uk is a static website hosted on GitHub Pages. We do not use tracking cookies, advertising cookies, or analytics platforms that set cookies. The following limited cookies may be set:

Third-party cookies

When you use external services linked from our site (Gumroad for purchases, Mailerlite for email sign-up, Formspree for contact forms), those services may set their own cookies. Their respective privacy and cookie policies apply.

Managing cookies

You can control and delete cookies through your browser settings. Disabling cookies will not affect your ability to read content on this site, though some form functionality may be limited.
